# Model policy

The screen routes a business first, tests the availability and quality of inputs next, then calculates every method that passes. The lead model is a presentation choice within the business archetype, not an average of unrelated values or a portfolio signal. Rank by gap only after comparing like models and inspecting the assumptions. All values are for the issuer’s common equity in USD; annual statement metrics stay in reporting currency until a comparison requires FX.

| Business or condition | Lead and secondary lenses | Decision rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deposit bank, broker, insurer | Multistage RI, justified P/B, P/B peers; DDM as a check | Require common book and income. Bank deposits and insurance liabilities are operating inputs; no industrial EV/FCFF. Check the equity roll-forward and preferred capital. |
| Operating company | FCFF, relevant peers, then FCFE or RI when eligible | Require coherent sales, EBIT, investment and debt/cash; positive operating economics unless a cycle model supports recovery. DDM is a secondary check. |
| Commodity and cyclical | Midcycle FCFF, cycle-matched EV/EBIT or EV/sales | Need at least five consecutive prior annual margins and positive median EBIT. No carry-forward of peak earnings. |
| Utility with established payout | Multistage DDM or Gordon DDM, then FCFF | Four or more paid annual dividends without a >20% cut, positive earnings, 10–90% payout, and no dominant buybacks. |
| Holding company and REIT | Adjusted NAV | Require date-stamped fair assets and senior claims. REIT AFFO and cap-rate models need separate inputs. |
| Loss-making company | EV/sales or EV/positive EBIT with comparable peers; recovery requires an explicit economics case | P/E is unavailable for zero or negative earnings. Positive book alone does not establish realizable NAV. |
| Missing or unclassified | Filing and coverage row | Unknown industry does not silently become an industrial company; missing facts remain missing. |

**Observed multiples.** P/E uses positive common earnings; P/B uses positive common book. EV is equity cap plus reported book debt and senior equity claims less cash and tagged short-term investments. The screen includes reported minority and preferred equity, inferring minority where parent and total equity are both available. An untagged preferred/minority claim can still be absent from EV; review material claims, leases, pensions, associates, and non-operating assets before relying on the bridge. Captive lenders such as Toyota need an operating and finance split. Peers must have the same business group, reporting basis, usable positive denominator, and fiscal end within 180 days; with basic scale and profitability gates; three peers minimum, leave-one-out median and interquartile value range. Earnings, profitability and growth can still differ materially; the model is a comparison, not a target.

**Gordon, dividends and RI.** Gordon equity value is `D1 / (r_e − g)`; justified forward P/E is `payout / (r_e − g)`. Justified P/B is `(ROE1 − g) / (r_e − g)`, identical to the single-stage RI value divided by book. Multistage RI starts with common book, adds discounted `NI_t − r_e B_(t−1)`, and discounts terminal RI from the end of the five-year forecast. ROE fades toward a bounded historical median; terminal growth is funded by retention `g / ROE`. The historical book roll-forward flags an unexplained gap above 5%; this is a screening check, not full OCI or capital-action reconciliation. DDM fades historical growth and screens for payout coverage. Repurchases and retained cash are not dividends.

**FCFF and FCFE.** FCFF uses `EBIT × (1 − tax) + D&A − capex − Δ operating working capital`; terminal FCFF uses `NOPAT_(T+1) × (1 − g/ROIC)`. Discount at WACC, then add the equity bridge. Near-term sales growth and operating ratios come from the issuer; terminal ROIC, rate hurdles, terminal growth and fallbacks are versioned policy assumptions. FCFE uses CFO less cash investment plus net borrowing. The automated route requires US GAAP, complete financing evidence and stable leverage, then fades to `NI_(T+1) × (1 − g/ROE)` at the cost of equity. An issuer-specific FCFE forecast can override it if dated and source-linked. No assumption that absent financing tags equal zero.

**Cycle.** Compare current EBIT margin to the prior-year median and quartiles, using at least five preceding annual observations in the same currency. Applying scaled margins to current sales avoids mixing business size with cycle position. For FCFF, fade toward trough, median, and strong historical margins across five years and display a separate reported-margin case. A margin percentile is company-relative and does not identify a recession, date the next turn, or distinguish a permanent mix change from a cycle. M&A, changes in accounting and exceptional charges still need review.

**Sensitivity.** For discount models, vary the discount rate by ±150 basis points and terminal growth by ±50 basis points. These are scenario ranges, not confidence intervals. The preset discount rates are rough nominal USD-equivalent hurdles applied under a constant-FX scenario. The model does not estimate issuer betas, sovereign premia, local inflation differences, or a time-varying currency risk premium.
