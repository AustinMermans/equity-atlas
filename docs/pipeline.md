# Data and operations

1. Capture the universe and FX independently. The ranking is provider-defined global USD issuer market cap and delayed, with no synchronized exchange close guarantee. FX uses the most recent available Frankfurter observation on or before the UTC capture date, at most five calendar days old. The first load is a research snapshot rather than a trade-time quote feed.
2. Resolve listing aliases to issuer CIK or LEI. Exact SEC CIK duplicates cause the run to stop rather than double count. Non-SEC issuers without a validated key are visible with a gap; the provider symbol is a temporary identity, not a permanent key. The next security master should store exchange MIC, local symbol, ISIN, instrument/share class, issuer linkage, ADS ratio, FX and effective dates separately.
3. Prefer structured filing facts: [EDGAR Company Facts](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) for US GAAP and IFRS filers, [XBRL International](https://filings.xbrl.org/) ESEF JSON for supported LEIs. A small reviewed set of dated official issuer statements fills gaps; it expires under the same freshness rule. Latest usable annual statement wins. Manual observations are source-linked and tracked in `issuer-annual.json`; no program assumes these observations refresh themselves.
4. Select fiscal-year date, reporting currency, and accession before selecting concepts. Filter on filing availability and actual duration, exclude dimensional ESEF facts, resolve standard alias order, omit contradictory observations, preserve concept, filing URL, unit/currency and raw-content hash. SEC Company Facts omits custom tags, many inline-only facts, and some current filings. Dates from ESEF repository ingestion are conservative publication bounds when direct dates are absent. The annual as-of screen is not a point-in-time backtesting database: retroactive changes, restatements, survivorship and filing timestamps need dedicated treatment.
5. Compute model applicability and readiness separately. Statements older than 465 days do not feed valuations. Comparable-company joins use pre-indexed groups; the source cache is hashed, throttled, and reused across universe sizes. Cache files, receipts, snapshots and local overrides stay in ignored directories. For larger runs, ingest bulk SEC Company Facts where reachable and store normalized observations in partitioned tables keyed by issuer, filing and period. Migrate the static snapshot to per-issuer JSON shards or a query API before shipping many thousands of rows.
6. Publish the official filings and exchange data using a separate export. Tests and an 80% fresh-coverage gate protect the existing deployment from poor source responses. A reviewed SEC-only seed exists for manual recovery if public runners cannot reach their sources. The seed cannot be mistaken for a new refresh; its date travels with it.

The next gate before production at 1,000 is ≥90% recent annual filings across the largest 100 and ≥85% in the expansion, verified venue/share-class IDs, representative review in each major region and business group, and no systematic denominator or cash-flow bridge errors. Prefer a license that covers global issuer fundamentals, current/delayed quotes, corporate actions and public display. Then add quarterly LTM with filing timestamps, region-specific risk rates, segment/SOTP, capital-return histories, cyclicality by driver and persisted data revisions. The ranking parser accepts top 1,000, top 2,000 and `--floor 800000000`; these are supported input sizes, not proven valuation coverage.

Sources: [SEC ticker and fair access notes](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data), [CompaniesMarketCap download](https://companiesmarketcap.com/?download=csv) and [terms](https://companiesmarketcap.com/terms-of-service/), [Frankfurter API](https://frankfurter.dev/), [XBRL International API](https://filings.xbrl.org/api/filings). The SEC ticker file returned HTTP 403 in the pilot; the cache used the [sec-cik-mapper mirror](https://github.com/jadchaar/sec-cik-mapper), with overrides verified against SEC or GLEIF primary identifiers. Legacy ADR facts are subject to staleness checks.

## Open market build

The scheduled Pages build downloads the Taiwan Stock Exchange daily close and listed-issuer registry. It joins by exchange code, excludes fund codes, and ranks 50 common-equity capitalization estimates within the exchange. Capitalization is close times reported issued ordinary shares. It can differ from an outstanding-share capitalization if an issuer has treasury stock, and the share registry carries its own report date. The Taiwan central bank converts TWD at its same-date interbank close. Exchange P/E and P/B come from the dated TWSE report after matching the closing price. These are Taiwan exchange ranks. SEC issuers outside this price coverage retain their filing results.

Sources: [TWSE closes](https://data.gov.tw/en/datasets/11549), [issuer registry](https://data.gov.tw/en/datasets/18419), [exchange multiples](https://data.gov.tw/dataset/11764), [Open Government Data License](https://data.gov.tw/en/license), and [Taiwan central bank FX](https://www.cbc.gov.tw/en/cp-510-1865-DA249-2.html).

The SEC build checks the accession and report date against Submissions, then extracts annual, standalone-quarter and year-to-date fields separately. It requests the extracted XBRL instance if the latest annual period is absent from Company Facts. An archive error leaves a visible source gap. Company-specific tags, dimensional disclosures and untagged line items need individual mappings and document reconciliation. The reviewed TSMC 20-F fallback uses IASB IFRS amounts and is reported separately from automated EDGAR coverage. Annual cash reconciliation checks the selected filing’s cash-flow total against balance-sheet cash and its filed restrictions. It uses standard XBRL concepts where the amounts reconcile and reviewed filing scopes for segregated customer and clearing balances. The published evidence keeps those components separate. A same-filing cash-flow difference without verified components is published as pending verification and counted separately. Run `python3 scripts/audit_edgar.py data/public-site/snapshot.json` to compare the November 2026 audit cohort against amounts independently checked in the filings.

Reviewed borrowing profiles recover carrying amounts from consolidated balance-sheet lines and exact note facts. They add disjoint current and noncurrent borrowing lines and include explicitly identified US GAAP finance leases where those are outside the borrowing lines. Separately reported IFRS lease balances can be shown alongside financial debt. The extract retains the filed components and rejects additional borrowing lines whose scope has not been reviewed. For banks and brokers with reviewed balance-sheet funding lines, the export reports those lines as funding disclosures and tracks them separately from total borrowing coverage. When an audited note supplies the consolidated amount across measurement categories, the total uses the note components and excludes duplicate funding lines. Berkshire’s rule reconciles both business-group note totals to the corresponding consolidated balance-sheet lines in the selected filing. A zero from the Palantir debt note requires the matching annual balance sheet and an explicit year-end zero. Palantir and HSBC’s public PDF copies are accession-specific. HSBC’s dedicated balance-sheet funding lines remain eligible in later filings under the recurring profile. JPMorgan uses the two standard borrowing concepts from the selected annual accession, with consolidated assets, liabilities and equity checked against that source. PDD uses its filed RMB convertible-bond cell and accepts an explicit dash as zero only when the classified current liabilities reconcile. MUFG separately reports long-term debt, other short-term borrowings and trust-account funding. Its trust note and other short-term borrowings must reconcile to the combined balance-sheet line.

China Mobile’s post-EDGAR route queries the HKEX annual report index for stock code 00941. The filing date in the index must match the PDF path, and the report must identify China Mobile and the selected fiscal year. The importer keeps the current and comparative RMB columns, verifies the balance sheet, income attribution, cash bridge and borrowing roll-forward, and links the original PDF. Bank borrowings, lease liabilities and the combined investing outflow are separate facts. The combined investing outflow includes other non-current assets, so it does not populate model capex. Public coverage counts HKEX filings separately from the active SEC cohort. The historical EDGAR periods and Form 15F documents remain linked.

PetroChina uses the same HKEX discovery route with stock code 00857. The importer selects the audited IFRS section in the dual-basis report and reconciles both fiscal columns. It checks current maturities and standalone short-term borrowings against the balance sheet and the separate breakdown by instrument. Lease liabilities and asset retirement obligations have separate fields. The cash-flow capital expenditures, segment capital expenditures and combined intangible and other asset purchases remain distinct. The disclosed PP&E impairment is separated from reported depreciation, depletion and amortisation.

## Annual cash distributions

The refresh checks the selected annual filing's financing cash flows and, where needed, its equity statement or dividend note. It keeps ordinary dividends, preferred distributions, hybrid coupons, subsidiary payments and noncash distributions separate. CIBC's share-class and capital-note payments must sum to its financing total. Sony uses effective payment dates from its common-stock note and reports any residual against consolidated cash dividends separately. HDFC uses the exact SEC-derived consolidated cash payment and the noncontrolling-interest dividend cell, with the resulting parent amount checked against the rounded equity statement.

The profiles run again when EDGAR selects a new annual accession. Filing identity, reporting units, statement scope and the relevant accounting checks must pass before the new amounts feed the model. An unfamiliar layout raises a dividend scope gap and blocks dividend valuation for that filing. A combined line stays available as filed when the common-stock portion is unverified. Model readiness still requires a sufficient history of comparable common cash dividends.

The cash-flow parser uses the financing header or the preceding investing subtotal to find the financing lines. Rio Tinto reports its subsidiary dividends in operating activities, and TotalEnergies reports hybrid coupons separately from parent dividends. Equinor's generic financing outflow must agree with its filed ordinary-share dividend disclosure. AstraZeneca's note ties the ordinary payment, currency effects, hedge receipts and subsidiary payments to the cash-flow total. Tagged equity-note amounts stay in a separate field because recognition and cash settlement can occur in different periods. The SEC taxonomy supplies explicitly scoped preferred and noncontrolling cash payments directly when the registrant reports those concepts.

## Borrowing cash flows

The annual build reads borrowing cash flows from the selected filing's consolidated financing section. The parsed rows must add to the filed financing subtotal. The reader checks reported signs and cross-checks exact Company Facts observations when available. It uses each face line once, including net commercial paper lines, and keeps debt issuance costs, lease principal and payments on financed assets separate. The borrowing field measures disclosed cash issuance and repayments. Unresolved financing lines are shown with their filed signed amounts, and identified borrowing flows remain visible as a partial subtotal.

The local valuation run reads the last three annual periods from their own filings. FCFE requires a reconciled borrowing amount for all three, then deducts any separately reported finance-lease principal and debt issuance costs. A same-period SEC-tagged finance-lease payment can come from the lease note when the cash-flow statement groups it. When the note and a separate cash-flow line both report the payment, the amounts must agree and the model deducts it once. A disclosed finance-lease liability with no principal payment evidence blocks FCFE. Financed-asset payments already included in cash investment are counted once. When the financing scope or its relationship to cash investment is unresolved, the model remains unavailable for that series.

An issuer-specific financing label is classified only when the tag and filed label both match the reviewed scope. Where an annual filing combines several kinds of financing in one row, the reader checks its same-filing note before allocating it. Equinor's note separates current debt, collateral and other movements and preserves the disclosed USD-million rounding difference. Alphabet's lease note identifies amounts already included in debt repayments and splits principal and prepayments. FCFE deducts both cash payments once. Costco's lease note reduces the unresolved financing balance by the verified lease principal and leaves any remainder visible.
